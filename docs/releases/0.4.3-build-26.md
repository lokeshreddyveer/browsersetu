# Browser Setu 0.4.3 (build 26)

## What's new
- **Four new layouts.** Hybrid keeps a full toolbar over the page with your tabs in a sidebar. Canvas is just the page, with the address on ⌘L and your tabs on ⇧⌘A. Research Desk puts two pages side by side. Workspace Deck lists every workspace first. They are grouped with the others under Everyday, Organized, Immersive and Productivity.
- **Tab groups in every tab strip.** Groups now show inline in Classic, Minimal and One Row, with colour, collapse and drag in and out, as they do in the sidebar.
- **A calmer sidebar.** Workspaces, search and Essentials stay in place while your pinned tabs, open tabs and groups scroll together. Essentials take at most two rows. Floating's sidebar also opens from the keyboard.
- **Full screen keeps your toolbar.** Your layout stays as it is in full screen, and Esc leaves it when nothing else is open.
- **Private tabs are marked everywhere.** A private tab is clearly marked in every layout, and moving a tab between windows keeps its privacy.
- **iPad and iPhone.** The iPad sidebar steps aside in narrow windows and its toolbar folds into More. Every iPhone layout, including the compact ones, reaches all your tabs from the menu.

## Carried from 0.4.2
- Video download and media processing tools are optional downloads (Settings > Media, or the optional Setup step), shown with their size first; nothing downloads without your yes. They are not part of the app.
- A small built-in JavaScript runner (sandboxed) answers YouTube's puzzles; no Node or Deno is needed.
