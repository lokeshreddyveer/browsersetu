# Browser Setu 0.3.2 (build 7)

Mac: notarized, stapled DMG. iPhone/iPad: TestFlight build 7. Built 17 October 2026.

## Fixed
- **Videos and Shorts starting by themselves while you scroll** (reported on YouTube on iPhone). A page can now start media only after a real tap or key press (and for six seconds after one). On by default; Settings › Privacy › Pages, with a per-site "let this site start video by itself" in Site Privacy and in Repair This Page. Playlists and "Autoplay next" wait for a tap.
- **New-tab search suggestions drawn under the favorites and cards** (Mac): the dropdown is now above the page.
- **Split view:** two panels share the window exactly 50-50; three are one tall page and two stacked; four are a 2×2 grid; dividers can be dragged, a double-click makes them equal.

## Checked
- 13 tests in the page-privacy classes pass (one skipped on purpose: the mute-and-skip path of YouTube ad skipping, because the test host cannot load media), including new tests that a page's `play()` is refused without a tap, that the autoplay attribute is removed, and that an exception list and the off switch leave playback alone.
- Looked at on screen on the Mac: the new-tab suggestions, and the two-, three- and four-panel split.
- In the iPhone simulator, a muted autoplay video and an autoplay sound clip both stayed paused.

## Not checked
- The scrolling bug itself on an iPhone with a signed-in YouTube feed (the simulator is signed out, and touch scrolling cannot be scripted there). Please scroll the YouTube home feed on the new build and tell me if anything still starts by itself.
- The "tap, then the page opens and plays" path on YouTube: the six-second window is a judgement, not a measurement.
